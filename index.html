How each lesson works

  1. Guess the answer to a puzzle first.
  2. Play with the model or slider in each chapter.
  3. Answer the quiz without scrolling up.
  4. Explain it to a friend in 5 sentences.

Every lesson also asks about lessons from 1, 7 and 30 days ago, so you keep what you learn.
